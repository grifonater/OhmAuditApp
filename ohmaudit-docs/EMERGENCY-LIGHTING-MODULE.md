# Emergency Lighting module

An Emergency Lighting System is the parent Asset. High-volume fittings are Inventory Items arranged in optional building/floor/room/zone hierarchy. Key switches use flexible group relationships; self-test fittings need not have a switch.

Functional and duration templates remain distinct. Bulk pass at room or switch-group level writes individual results and supports exceptions. Customer inspection and defect/maintenance reports are views of one inspection dataset.
