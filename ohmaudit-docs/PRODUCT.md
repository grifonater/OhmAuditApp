# Product

Ohm Audit helps electrical contractors manage assets, visits, inspections, servicing, defects, and durable compliance records with minimum engineer effort. The first specialist modules are EV Charging, Emergency Lighting, and Solar PV.

The primary navigation hierarchy is Organisation → Customer → Site → assets, visits, inspections, and documents. Modules enrich a site; they do not fragment the product. Ohm Audit excludes contractor-to-customer quoting, invoicing, accounting, payroll, and a customer portal from initial scope.

## Acceptance principles

Field work must remain practical on a small phone, offline, in difficult conditions. Office staff must find a precise asset or certificate within seconds even across hundreds of sites. History is archived or decommissioned, never silently deleted.
